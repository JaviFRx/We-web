Explain and Present Solutions

Organize a session in which each team can present a demo of their solution(s).

Computational Thinking Skills

Enabeling students to describe a concept (Abstraction)

Abstraction is the main skill being developed during this phase. Therefore, the focus is on enabling your students to present their solutions. Have each student explain their solution using correct vocabulary and the right level of detail.

Make sure they can:

  • Explain the most important part of their solution

  • Give enough details to enhance comprehension of the concepts involved

  • Explain how their solution met the initial criteria

Assessing the Share Phase

During the Share phase, make sure the student is able to describe their solution using correct vocabulary and the right level of detail.

Feedback can be given on a scale of 1 to 4:

  1. The student does not use evidence from his or her findings in connection with ideas shared during the presentation. The student does not follow established guidelines.

  2. The student uses some evidence from his or her findings, but the justification is limited. Established guidelines are generally followed but may be lacking in one or more areas.

  3. The student adequately provides evidence to justify his or her findings and follows established guidelines for presenting.

  4. The student fully discusses his or her findings and thoroughly utilizes appropriate evidence to justify their reasoning while following all established guidelines.

Specific rubrics for assessing computational thinking skills can be found in the Teacher guide.
