Explain and Present Solutions

Organise a session in which each team can present a demonstration of their solution(s).

Computational Thinking Skills

Enabling the pupils to describe a concept (Abstraction)

Abstraction is the main skill that is being developed during this phase. Therefore, the focus is on enabling your pupils to present their solutions. Have each pupil explain their solution using the correct vocabulary and the right level of detail.

Make sure that they can:

  • Explain the most important part of their solution

  • Give enough details to enhance their comprehension of the concepts involved

  • Explain how their solution met the initial criteria

Assessing the Share Phase

During the Share phase, make sure that the pupil is able to describe their solution using the correct vocabulary and the right level of detail.

Feedback can be given on a scale of 1 to 4:

  1. The pupil does not use evidence from their findings in connection with the ideas that are shared during the presentation. The pupil does not follow the established guidelines.

  2. The pupil uses some evidence from their findings, but the justification is limited. Established guidelines are generally followed but may be lacking in one or more areas.

  3. The pupil adequately provides evidence in order to justify their findings and follows the established guidelines for presenting.

  4. The pupil fully discusses their findings and thoroughly utilises appropriate evidence in order to justify their reasoning while following all of the established guidelines.

Specific rubrics for assessing computational thinking skills can be found in the curriculum document.
