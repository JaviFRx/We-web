Type of Project

This project is a design brief focusing on the development of computational thinking skills. While this project will activate all five of these skills, an emphasis can be placed on the skills that are most relevant to you and your pupils.

In this project, the focus could be

  • Decomposition: simplifying the problem by identifying what actions need to be taken at each stage of volcanic activity

  • Generalisation: finding patterns within the different stages of volcanic activity and defining what actions need to be taken

  • Algorithmic Thinking: creating a program to activate the alarm

  • Evaluation: improving the solution until the alarm works consistently

  • Abstraction: explaining the solution with the right level of detail

Planning a Teaching Sequence

Preparation: 30 Minutes

  • Read the project and the relevant Teacher Assistant material.

  • Define how you would like to introduce this project. You can use the video provided with the project or use your own material.

  • Determine the end result of this project. For example, define the parameters of your pupils’ presentations and the specific elements that they should include in their documents.

Explore Phase: 10–45 Minutes

  • Start the project by showing the video or presenting your own material on the subject.

  • Have a group discussion.

  • Allow your pupils to document their work using the Documentation tool.

Create Phase: 20 Minutes

  • Have your pupils build the first model by following the building instructions.

  • Let them program the model using the sample program.

  • Allow them time to understand the model that they will be using.

Test Phase: 25 Minutes

  • Read the first task with your pupils.

  • Allow enough time so that they can plan, try and test their solution.

  • Read the second task with your pupils.

  • Allow enough time so that they can plan, try and test their solution.

Create More Phase (Optional): 45–60 Minutes

  • If you wish to do so, you may use this extra layer of the project for further learning or for older pupils.

Share Phase: 45 Minutes or More

  • Make sure that your pupils document their work.

  • Use different ways in order to let your pupils share what they have learned and their reflections on these experiences.

  • Have your pupils create their final reports and present their projects.
