Communication between people has always been a fundamental part of our existence. Not long ago, before satellites and mobile phones were invented, various ingenious devices were used in order to exchange information.

For a long time, the only way to send a message from one place to another was to write it down and have someone deliver the message. In order to send messages more effectively, new ways of transferring messages, such as using electric current and wave signals, were explored. But the challenge was not only to create a device to send and receive signals, but also to develop a way of converting a common message into signals. Morse code is one of the most well-known ways to do this. Morse code transcribes each letter into a series of short and long signals.

Vocabulary Words

Communication Satellite

An object orbiting around the Earth that can relay a signal from one antenna to another.

Binary

Something that has two possible states (e.g., on or off, true or false, molten or frozen, the presence or absence of a signal, etc.).
