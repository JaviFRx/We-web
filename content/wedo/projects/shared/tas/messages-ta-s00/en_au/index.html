Type of Project

This project is a design brief focusing on the development of computational thinking skills. While this project will activate all five of these skills, an emphasis can be placed on the skills that are most relevant to you and your students.

In this project, the focus could be:

  • Decomposition: simplifying the problem by identifying various ways in which light or sound can be used to send a signal

  • Generalisation: finding patterns in Morse code in order to convert letters or words into a signal

  • Algorithmic Thinking: creating a program to convert a word into a signal

  • Evaluation: improving the solution until a message can be sent from one student to another

  • Abstraction: explaining the solution with the right level of detail

Planning a Teaching Sequence

Preparation: 30 Minutes

  • Read the project and the relevant Teacher Assistant material.

  • Define how you would like to introduce this project. You can use the video provided with the project or use your own material.

  • Determine the end result of this project. For example, define the parameters of your students’ presentations and the specific elements that they should include in their documents.

Explore Phase: 10–45 Minutes

  • Start the project by showing the video or presenting your own material on the subject.

  • Have a group discussion.

  • Allow your students to document their work using the Documentation tool.

Create Phase: 20 Minutes

  • Have your students build the first model by following the building instructions.

  • Let them program the model using the sample program.

  • Allow them time to understand the model that they will be using.

Test Phase: 25 Minutes

  • Read the first task with your students.

  • Allow enough time so that they can plan, try and test their solution.

  • Read the second task with your students.

  • Allow enough time so that they can plan, try and test their solution.

Create More Phase (Optional): 45–60 Minutes

  • If you wish to do so, you may use this extra layer of the project for further learning or for older students.

Share Phase: 45 Minutes or More

  • Make sure that your students document their work.

  • Use different ways in order to let your students share what they have learned and their reflections on these experiences.

  • Have your students create their final reports and present their projects.
