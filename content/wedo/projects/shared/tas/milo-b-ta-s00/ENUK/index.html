Overview of the Project

In this section of the project, the pupils will be introduced to the Motion Sensor, which they will use in order to detect the presence of a special plant specimen.

Type of Project

This Getting Started Project will enable you and your pupils to get in touch with more elements of the WeDo 2.0 projects, including:

  • Exploring ways to use the Motion Sensor

  • Recording a video with the Capture tool

Duration

Approx. 20 minutes
