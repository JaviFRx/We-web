Differentiation Opportunities

Here are two alternative ways to conduct this phase of the project:

  • Have the students design antennas that they can program to rotate and aim in the direction in which they would like to send or receive a message.

  • Have the students explore how satellites work by creating a model of a satellite to explain how they relay messages.
